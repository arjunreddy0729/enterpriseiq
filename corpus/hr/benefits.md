# Employee Benefits Guide

**Audience:** all Northwind employees
**Owner:** Aisha Nkemelu, HR Business Partner (aisha.nkemelu@northwind.example)
**Approver:** Marcus Webb, Director of People Operations

What Northwind Systems, Inc. provides beyond salary: health coverage, retirement,
insurance, stipends, and support programs, for regular employees in Austin (HQ),
Denver, and Dublin. Time off is not covered here — see leave-policy.md. Plan
documents always control; where this guide and a carrier's plan document disagree,
the plan document is correct and People Operations will reconcile it.

---

## 1. Health Coverage

Northwind offers medical, dental, and vision. The employer contribution is the
same across all three:

| Who is covered | Northwind pays | You pay |
|----------------|----------------|---------|
| Employee | **90%** of the premium | 10% |
| Dependents (spouse/partner, children) | **70%** of the premium | 30% |

Your share is deducted pre-tax through payroll. There is no waiting period —
coverage is effective on your **start date**, not the first of the following
month.

US employees choose between plan tiers at enrollment; tiers, deductibles, and
network details are in the enrollment packet published each autumn. Dental and
vision are single-plan and automatic if elected. Dublin-based employees are
enrolled in a locally administered medical scheme with the same 90% / 70% split,
with dental and vision bundled in rather than elected separately.

Eligible dependents are your spouse or domestic partner and children up to the
plan's age limit. Domestic partners are covered on the same terms as spouses, and
documentation is requested once, at enrollment, and never again.

## 2. Retirement

### 2.1 401(k) — US employees

- Northwind matches **4%** of eligible compensation.
- The match is **immediately vested**. No vesting schedule, no cliff, nothing to
  forfeit if you leave. The money is yours the day it lands.
- You are eligible from your first paycheck; there is no waiting period.
- Traditional (pre-tax) and Roth (post-tax) contributions are both supported, and
  you can split between them.
- Annual contribution limits are set by statute; the current figure is in the
  provider portal.

```text
Base salary                         $150,000
Your contribution at 4%             $  6,000
Northwind match at 4%               $  6,000   <- immediately vested
--------------------------------------------
Annual retirement contribution      $ 12,000
```

Northwind matches what you put in, up to 4%. Contributing 2% gets a 2% match, not
4%.

### 2.2 Dublin employees

Ireland-based employees participate in a locally administered retirement
arrangement of equivalent employer value, coordinated by People Operations. The
401(k) is a US plan and is not available outside the US.

## 3. Stipends

| Stipend | Amount | Frequency | Claim category |
|---------|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Learning and development | **$1,500** | Per calendar year | `L&D` |
| Wellness | **$75** | Per month | `Wellness` |
| Home office | **$500** | One time, at hire | `Home Office` |

All three are reimbursements, not allowances — you pay, then claim through
NorthPay Expense. Standard expense rules apply: receipts are required for anything
over **$25**, and claims must be submitted **within 30 days** of the expense date.
Reimbursements are paid on the two monthly runs, on the 15th and the last business
day of the month.

### 3.1 Learning and development — $1,500 per year

For anything that makes you better at your job, or at the job you want next.
Manager approval is required before you spend.

**In scope:** conference tickets, workshops and training courses, online course
subscriptions, certification exam fees, technical and professional books,
professional association dues, language tuition supporting cross-office
collaboration, and coaching from a credentialed coach.

**Out of scope:** hardware and peripherals, which are equipment requests rather
than L&D; travel and accommodation for a conference, which follow the Finance
travel policy and its airfare and hotel caps and are **not** charged against the
$1,500; and degree programs, which People Operations handles separately.

The budget is **per calendar year and does not carry over**. Unused balance on
31 December is gone. It is prorated for employees who join partway through a year.

### 3.2 Wellness — $75 per month

Gym and studio memberships, fitness classes, home equipment, therapy and
meditation apps, sports registration fees, ergonomic accessories. No manager
approval needed. The monthly amount does not accumulate — an unclaimed month is
not added to the next one — so most people set up a recurring claim and forget
about it.

### 3.3 Home office — $500 one time

A single reimbursement for setting up your working space: desk, chair, monitor
arm, lighting, or similar. Available once per employee, not once per year, and it
does not reset on relocation. Laptop, monitor, keyboard, and mouse come from IT
and are not bought from this stipend — see remote-work-policy.md for the standard
equipment list.

## 4. Insurance

**Life and AD&D.** Basic life and accidental death and dismemberment cover at
**two times base salary**, capped at $500,000, at no cost to you. Supplemental
cover for yourself and dependents can be bought through payroll at enrollment;
amounts above the guaranteed issue threshold require carrier underwriting.

Name a beneficiary during onboarding. Roughly a quarter of employees never do, and
it is the most common gap People Operations finds in a records audit. Review it
after any life event.

**Disability.** Both employer paid:

| Cover | Benefit | Starts |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Short-term disability | 60% of base salary, up to 12 weeks | After a 7-day elimination period |
| Long-term disability | 60% of base salary | When short-term disability is exhausted |

Disability leave is coordinated with sick leave under leave-policy.md. If you are
out more than five consecutive days, People Operations gets involved and sequences
disability, statutory entitlements, and your return-to-work plan for you. Dublin
employees have equivalent income-protection cover under the local scheme.

## 5. Employee Assistance Program

The EAP is confidential, free, and available to you and members of your household
from day one:

- Short-term counselling — **8 sessions per issue, per year**, in person or
  virtual
- Crisis support, 24 hours a day
- Legal and financial consultations
- Childcare and eldercare referrals
- Relocation and immigration support resources

Northwind receives aggregate usage counts only — no names, no topics, no session
details — and no manager is notified that you have used it.

## 6. Open Enrollment

Open enrollment runs for **two weeks each November**, with elections effective
**1 January**.

| Step | Timing |
|------|--------|
| Enrollment packet published | Late October |
| Live Q&A sessions (Austin, Denver, Dublin) | First week of November |
| Enrollment window open | Two weeks in November |
| Confirmation statements issued | Early December |
| New elections effective | 1 January |

Two things to know. First, enrollment is not passive for everything: medical,
dental, and vision roll over if you take no action, but flexible spending account
elections do **not** and lapse to zero unless re-elected. Second, once the window
closes elections are locked until the next open enrollment, unless you have a
qualifying life event.

New hires get a 30-day enrollment window from their start date, independent of the
annual cycle.

## 7. Qualifying Life Events

A qualifying life event lets you change elections outside open enrollment. You have
**30 days** from the date of the event to submit the change in People Hub with
supporting documentation. After 30 days you wait for the next open enrollment —
this deadline comes from plan rules and People Operations cannot extend it.

Qualifying events include:

- Marriage, divorce, legal separation, or entry into or dissolution of a domestic
  partnership
- Birth, adoption, or placement of a child
- Death of a spouse, partner, or dependent
- A dependent gaining or losing eligibility, for example a child reaching the plan
  age limit
- Your spouse or partner gaining or losing coverage through their own employer
- A change in your employment status affecting benefits eligibility
- A permanent relocation out of a plan's service area

Changes are effective the date of the event for births, adoptions, and marriages,
and the first of the following month otherwise.

## 8. When You Leave

- Health coverage runs through the **last day of the month** in which employment
  ends. Continuation options are in your offboarding packet.
- The 401(k) balance, including all employer match, is fully yours to keep or roll
  over.
- Life and disability cover ends on your last day; conversion options are in the
  plan document.
- Stipend claims must be submitted before your last working day, within the normal
  30-day window.
- The EAP remains available to you for 90 days after separation.

## 9. Common Questions

**Can I trade an unused stipend for cash?** No. All three are reimbursements
against actual spend.

**Does the learning stipend cover conference travel?** No. The ticket comes out of
the $1,500; flights and hotel go through the Finance travel policy.

**My partner's employer has better medical. Can I waive coverage?** Yes, during
enrollment or within 30 days of hire. There is no cash payment in lieu.

**I am on parental leave. Do my benefits continue?** Yes, unchanged, with the
employer contribution unchanged, for the full 16 or 8 weeks. See leave-policy.md.

**Who do I contact?** Benefits questions go to People Operations. Anything about
an individual claim goes to the carrier — Northwind cannot see or influence claim
decisions.
