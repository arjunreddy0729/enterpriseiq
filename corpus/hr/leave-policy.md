# Leave and Time Off Policy

**Audience:** all Northwind employees
**Owner:** Marcus Webb, Director of People Operations (marcus.webb@northwind.example)
**Co-owner:** Aisha Nkemelu, HR Business Partner (aisha.nkemelu@northwind.example)

This policy covers paid time off, sick leave, parental leave, and every other
category of leave available to regular employees at Northwind Systems, Inc. Where
it conflicts with local law in your work location, local law wins and People
Operations will tell you what the difference is — you do not need to work that out
yourself. Figures assume a regular full-time employee; part-time employees accrue
and are granted pro rata.

## 1. Paid Time Off

### 1.1 Accrual

Every full-time employee accrues **20 days of PTO per year**. Accrual is monthly
and even — **1.667 days per month**, credited on the last calendar day of the
month. Nothing is front-loaded. On a semi-monthly payroll cadence that is roughly
6.67 hours per pay period. Your balance in People Hub is the authoritative number.
New hires accrue from their start date, prorated for the first partial month.

### 1.2 Going negative

You may go up to **5 days negative** with manager approval, so that someone who
joins in February can still take a week off in May. Beyond 5 days negative it
becomes an unpaid leave request and People Operations has to be involved. A
negative balance at termination is recovered from the final paycheck where local
law permits.

### 1.3 Carryover

You may carry a **maximum of 5 days** into the following calendar year, measured
at end of day **31 December**. Anything above 5 days is forfeited on 1 January and
cannot be reinstated, converted to cash, or banked.

Balance warnings go out in November to anyone above 10 days, and again in the
first week of December. Managers are expected to actively push people to book
time. If someone on your team loses days at year end, that is a management
failure, not an employee failure. Carryover is not extended for people who could
not take leave because of workload; the fix for that situation is to take the
leave.

### 1.4 Rest expectations

- Take at least one block of **five consecutive working days** per year. Long
  weekends are useful, but they are not rest.
- While on PTO you are not expected to read messages, join meetings, or review
  code. If you are on the on-call rotation, arrange coverage before you go — see
  remote-work-policy.md.

## 2. Company Holidays

Northwind observes **11 company holidays** per year. The US schedule:

1. New Year's Day
2. Presidents' Day
3. Memorial Day
4. Juneteenth
5. Independence Day
6. Labor Day
7. Thanksgiving Day
8. Day after Thanksgiving
9. Christmas Eve
10. Christmas Day
11. New Year's Eve

Dublin-based employees observe local public holidays instead, to the same total of
11 days; the Dublin schedule is published each November for the following year.

Company holidays do not draw down PTO. Where a holiday falls on a weekend, the
observed day is in the annual calendar. If you work a company holiday because you
are on-call and get paged, take an equivalent day back within the same quarter and
record it in People Hub as holiday-in-lieu, not PTO.

## 3. Sick Leave

Sick leave is **unlimited** and entirely separate from PTO. It covers your own
illness or injury, medical and dental appointments, mental-health days, and caring
for an immediate family member who is ill.

The rules are deliberately short:

- **Notify your manager.** A message before the start of your working day is
  enough. You do not need to explain the diagnosis and your manager should not
  ask.
- **Log it in People Hub** as sick leave so coverage is visible. It does not touch
  your PTO balance.
- **A doctor's note is required only after 5 consecutive days** of absence. Under
  five days, no documentation is requested, ever.

Absences beyond five consecutive days move into a managed conversation with People
Operations, who will sequence short-term disability, any statutory entitlement,
and a return-to-work plan. Your manager is not the right person to handle that;
hand it over early. Unlimited does not mean unmonitored in the aggregate, but
People Operations only looks at patterns when a manager raises a concern, and that
conversation is about support first.

## 4. Parental Leave

Fully paid, for birth, adoption, surrogacy, and foster placement.

| Role | Duration | Pay |
|------|----------|-----|
| Primary caregiver | 16 weeks | 100% of base salary |
| Secondary caregiver | 8 weeks | 100% of base salary |

### 4.1 Eligibility and mechanics

- Available from day one. There is no tenure requirement.
- You designate yourself primary or secondary. The designation is not tied to
  gender, and two Northwind employees may each take their respective entitlement.
- Leave must begin within the child's first 12 months, or within 12 months of
  placement.
- It may be taken in up to **two blocks** inside that window, minimum block two
  weeks. Single-day intermittent use is not supported.
- Benefits continue unchanged (benefits.md) and equity continues to vest.
- Bonus target and merit eligibility are not reduced. This is stated explicitly in
  compensation-policy.md and is not subject to manager discretion.

### 4.2 Statutory coordination

Where statutory or state-paid leave exists in your location, Northwind's payment
runs concurrently and tops up to 100% rather than stacking. Dublin-based employees
have statutory entitlements that interact with this policy; People Operations
sequences that for you.

### 4.3 Planning and return

Tell your manager and People Operations when you are comfortable — ideally by the
start of the second trimester or as soon as a placement date is known — so
coverage can be planned. A written handover is expected two weeks before leave
starts. Returning employees get a documented ramp of at least two weeks with
reduced scope and no production release ownership, and are **not** placed on the
on-call rotation in their first four weeks back.

## 5. Other Leave

**Bereavement.** Up to **5 paid days** for an immediate family member — spouse or
partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or an equivalent
relationship including in-law and chosen family. Up to **3 paid days** for
extended family or a close friend. Additional time is routinely granted where
travel is involved; ask, and the answer is usually yes. Not deducted from PTO, no
documentation required.

**Jury duty and civic obligations.** Jury service, witness summons, and voting
time are paid in full for the duration. Forward the summons to People Operations
and tell your manager. Any court stipend is yours to keep.

**Military leave.** Handled individually by People Operations in accordance with
applicable law. Contact aisha.nkemelu@northwind.example before committing to dates
with your manager.

**Unpaid leave of absence.** Where paid categories do not fit — extended family
care, education, a situation needing months rather than weeks — an unpaid leave
may be approved by your manager together with People Operations.

- Normally granted in increments of up to 12 weeks.
- Benefits continuation and cost sharing are confirmed in writing beforehand.
- PTO does not accrue during unpaid leave.
- Equity treatment for unpaid leave over 30 days is confirmed in writing at
  approval.
- Role hold-open commitments are stated in the approval, not assumed.

Northwind does not operate any leave category beyond those listed here. If you are
looking for something not described in this document, ask People Operations rather
than assuming an equivalent exists.

## 6. Requesting Time Off

1. Check your team's calendar for overlapping absences.
2. Submit the request in **People Hub** under the correct leave type.
3. Give reasonable notice — **two weeks** for absences of a week or more; same day
   is fine for a single day.
4. Your manager responds within **three business days**. A request left unanswered
   past three business days escalates automatically to the skip-level manager.
5. If you are in an on-call rotation, arrange the swap before approval, not after.

Declines need a reason. "Team capacity during a committed launch window" is a
reason. "Busy period" is not.

## 7. Coordination Windows

Northwind does not operate hard PTO blackouts. Two windows need extra
coordination:

- **20–31 December.** This overlaps the production deploy freeze, when no
  production deploys go out. It is one of the best times of year to be away and we
  encourage people to take it. The catch is that the on-call rotation still runs
  through it, and coverage for that window is finalised by **30 November**.
  Engineering managers own that schedule; Sofia Reyes coordinates for
  Infrastructure.
- **Late March.** Managers are in calibration and compensation conversations.
  Manager PTO in the final two weeks of March needs skip-level agreement, because
  outcomes are delivered in person before the 1 April effective date.

Separately: no production deploys go out after **16:00 on Friday**. Do not plan to
"just ship it before I leave" on a Friday afternoon ahead of a week off. Ship on
Wednesday, or hand it over.

## 8. Leave and Termination

- **Accrued and unused PTO is paid out** in the final paycheck at base salary
  rate, in accordance with applicable law. Carryover limits do not apply to the
  payout — you are paid for the balance you hold on your last day.
- A **negative PTO balance is recovered** from the final paycheck where law
  permits.
- Company holidays after your last working day are not paid.
- Unused sick leave has no cash value and is not paid out; this follows directly
  from sick leave being unlimited.
- Employees on parental leave who choose not to return are not asked to repay
  parental leave pay. There is no clawback.

Your last working day is your last day of active employment. PTO cannot be used to
extend a notice period without written People Operations agreement.
