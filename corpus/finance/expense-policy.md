# Travel and Expense Policy

**Owner:** Dana Okafor (Controller) · **Applies to:** all Northwind employees and
contractors who spend Northwind money · **Effective:** 1 January 2026

This is the *what* — what Northwind will pay for and up to how much. The *how* —
filing a report, approval routing, when the money lands — lives in
reimbursement.md. Purchasing goods and services on Northwind's behalf, as opposed
to spending on your own travel, is governed separately by procurement.

## The three rules that matter

1. **Spend it like it's yours, but travel like a professional.** We are not going
   to itemise your coffee. We do expect you to pick the reasonable option when
   two are in front of you.
2. **Document it.** Anything over $25 needs a receipt. No receipt, no
   reimbursement, and no amount of "I promise it was $180" changes that.
3. **File it within 30 days.** The clock starts on the expense date, not the day
   you got back.

If a situation genuinely isn't covered here, ask Finance *before* you spend, not
after. Retroactive approval is possible but it is a favour, not a process.

## What changed on 1 January 2026

The **domestic meal cap was raised from $75/day to $85/day**, effective
2026-01-01. Expenses incurred on or after that date use $85/day. Expenses
incurred in 2025 use the old $75/day cap even if you file them in January — the
cap that applies is the one in force on the **expense date**, not the submission
date. Older copies of this policy circulating in team wikis still say $75; they
are superseded.

## Meals

The **domestic cap is $85 per day**, covering all meals and non-alcoholic
beverages for that day combined. It is a ceiling, not an allowance — if you spend
$31 on a travel day, claim $31. There is no per-meal sub-cap: a $12 breakfast and
a $70 dinner is fine, a $70 breakfast and a $70 dinner is not.

The cap applies per person per day. If you pay for a colleague's meal, claim the
full amount, name the attendees in the report, and the cap is assessed against
the head count. A three-person dinner in Denver may reach $255.

Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client entertainment expense or an
approved team event. It is not reimbursable on solo travel. This is not a
temperance position; it is an audit position.

### International travel

Meals outside the United States are reimbursed against a **published per-diem for
the destination city**, maintained by Finance and shown in NorthPay Expense when
you select the destination. Dublin-based staff travelling within the EU use the
same mechanism. The domestic $85 figure does not apply abroad in either
direction — do not assume it is a floor.

Two ways to claim, chosen per trip, not per meal:

- **Actuals.** Itemise what you spent, up to the published per-diem, with receipts
  over $25. This is the default.
- **Flat per diem.** Claim the published daily rate without meal receipts. Travel
  dates and a boarding pass or hotel folio still required. Departure and return
  days are claimed at 75% of the rate.

Pick one method for the whole trip. Mixing them mid-trip is the single most
common reason an international report gets sent back.

## Air travel

- **Under 6 hours scheduled flight time: economy.** Basic economy fares that
  prohibit any change are discouraged for travel booked more than two weeks out;
  the change fee we avoid is rarely worth the flexibility we lose.
- **Over 6 hours scheduled flight time: premium economy is allowed.** Allowed, not
  required. The 6-hour test is per leg, measured on scheduled flight time for the
  longest single segment, not total door-to-door journey time.
- **Business class requires VP approval**, obtained in writing before booking.
  Attach the approval to the expense report. A business-class ticket booked first
  and justified afterwards will be reimbursed at the premium economy fare
  available at the time of booking, and the difference is yours.
- Book at least 14 days ahead where the trip is known. Last-minute fares are
  reimbursable but they show up in the travel variance Finance reports quarterly.
- Seat selection, one checked bag each way, and in-flight wifi on work travel are
  reimbursable. Lounge access, airline club memberships, and seat upgrades bought
  with cash at the gate are not.
- Loyalty points earned on Northwind-funded travel are yours. Do not, however,
  route through an inconvenient or more expensive itinerary to earn them.

Accessibility and medical needs override the cabin rules entirely. Talk to Marcus
Webb in People Operations rather than this document; no medical detail belongs in
an expense report.

## Lodging

Caps are **$250/night in US metro areas** and **$180/night elsewhere**, measured
on the room rate **before** tax and mandatory fees. Taxes, occupancy fees, and
resort fees are reimbursable on top of the cap; they are not discretionary and
pretending otherwise just makes people book worse hotels.

- Conference venue rates that exceed the cap are acceptable when the conference
  is the reason for the trip. Note the conference name in the report.
- If the whole market is above cap on your dates — a citywide event, a
  compressed booking window — book the reasonable option and add one line of
  explanation. Finance approves these routinely.
- Staying with friends or family instead of a hotel: claim nothing. We do not pay
  a "saved the company money" stipend.
- Long-stay apartments for trips over 14 nights often beat the nightly cap
  outright. Ask Finance before booking; these usually need a purchase order.
- Personal nights bracketing a business trip are your own cost. Split the folio.

## Ground transport

- **Personal vehicle: $0.67 per mile.** Log the date, the route, the business
  purpose, and the mileage. Fuel receipts are not claimable in addition to
  mileage; the rate already includes fuel and wear.
- Rideshare and taxis at actual cost. Airport-to-hotel, hotel-to-office, and
  client visits are all normal. The daily commute to your regular office is not,
  in any city, for anyone.
- Parking and tolls on business travel are reimbursable with a receipt over $25.
- Rental cars: mid-size or below unless you are carrying equipment or more than
  three people. Decline the rental company's collision waiver on domestic
  rentals — our corporate policy already covers it. Accept it abroad.
- Public transit is reimbursable and is usually the right answer in Dublin.

Parking tickets, speeding fines, and towing are never reimbursable. Neither is
the deductible on a personal vehicle accident that happened on a mileage trip.

## Receipts

**Receipts are required for any expense over $25.** For hotels, client
entertainment, airfare, and rental cars, a receipt is required regardless of
amount.

A valid receipt is itemised and shows the merchant, the date, the currency, and
the total. A credit card slip showing only a total is not itemised and is not
sufficient for a hotel folio or a restaurant bill over $75. Photos are fine;
image quality standards are in reimbursement.md.

If a receipt is genuinely lost, file a missing-receipt attestation in NorthPay
Expense with the amount, date, merchant, and business purpose. Two in a quarter
is a conversation with your manager. Finance tracks them.

## The 30-day deadline

**Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.** For multi-day
trips, the clock starts on the last day of the trip.

Reports filed past 30 days need director approval. Reports past 90 days need VP
Finance approval and, past the calendar year end, may not be reimbursable at all
because the books for that period are closed. This is an accounting constraint,
not a punishment — see revenue-recognition.md for why period boundaries are hard
edges in Finance.

## Corporate cards versus personal cards

- **Corporate cards are issued at manager level and above.** If you have one, use
  it for business spend. Do not use it for anything personal, not even with the
  intention of paying it back.
- **Everyone else pays with a personal card and gets reimbursed.** That is the
  normal path at Northwind and carries no stigma. Payment run timing is in
  reimbursement.md.
- If a personal-card purchase would cause you real hardship before the next
  payment run — a large airfare, a conference fee — contact
  finance-ap@northwind.example. Finance can pay the vendor directly or arrange an
  advance. Ask before you book.
- Corporate cardholders still file expense reports. The card statement is not a
  substitute for coding, business purpose, and receipts, and unreconciled card
  transactions over 45 days old are escalated to the cardholder's director.

## Not reimbursable

Not an exhaustive list, but it covers the recurring questions:

- Parking tickets, moving violations, and towing
- Airline lounge access, club memberships, and gate-purchased upgrades
- Personal travel legs, companion tickets, and personal hotel nights
- Alcohol on solo travel
- In-room minibar and pay-per-view
- Personal grooming, gym day passes, and spa charges
- Home internet and mobile phone plans (see remote-work-policy.md for what
  People Operations covers)
- Office furniture bought individually — the home-office stipend in benefits.md
  is the mechanism for that
- Charitable donations, political contributions, and gifts to public officials in
  any amount, ever
- Lost or stolen personal property
- Childcare and pet care during travel
- Fees for expedited passport or visa services, unless the trip was assigned with
  under three weeks' notice

## Client entertainment

Entertaining customers and prospects is legitimate spend and it is also the
category auditors read first. Rules:

- **Always attach a receipt**, regardless of amount.
- Record **every attendee by name and organisation**. "Client dinner, 6 people"
  will be rejected. "Dinner with four attendees from [customer], plus Dev Shah
  and me" is fine.
- Record the **business purpose** in a sentence — what was being discussed.
- A Northwind employee must be present. We do not fund customer meals we did not
  attend.
- Alcohol is reimbursable here, within reason and within the total.
- Entertainment over **$500** requires director approval before the event.
- **Gifts to customers are capped at $150 per recipient per year** and must be
  logged. Cash and cash equivalents, including gift cards, are prohibited as
  customer gifts without exception.
- Anything involving a government official, a public-sector customer, or a
  state-owned enterprise goes to Tom Lindqvist in Legal **before** it happens.
  Public-sector gift rules are stricter than ours and are not negotiable.

Client entertainment during a live sales negotiation is fine; timed to coincide
with a contract signature it looks like something else. Use judgement.

## Exceptions

Exceptions are granted, in writing, in advance, by:

| Situation | Approver |
|---|---|
| One-off overage under $500 | Your manager |
| Business class airfare | Any VP |
| Sustained above-cap lodging for a project | VP Finance (Ken Whitaker) |
| Anything above $25,000 in aggregate travel commitment | VP Finance |
| Novel category not covered here | Controller (Dana Okafor) |

Approvals live in the expense report as an attachment. An approval that exists
only in a chat thread does not exist as far as the annual audit is concerned.
